# com_servicedeskplus 1.31.0

Update from **System → Update → Extensions**, or install `com_servicedeskplus.zip` (attached below). No database changes.

## New
- **Cards above the lists** with totals; click a card to show that group:
  - *My Requests:* categories (for the chosen status and type).
  - *My Approvals:* Requests, Changes, Releases, Purchase orders.
  - *My Loans:* On loan, Due within 30 days, Overdue, Returned.
  - *Announcements:* announcement types.
- **Dashboard:** *Returned* in the My Loans card, and a colour key under *Open Requests by Priority*.

## Changed
- Loan names: **On loan**, **Due within 30 days**, **Overdue**, **Returned**.
